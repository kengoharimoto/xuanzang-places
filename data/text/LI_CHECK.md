# Li Rongxi (1996) against the Chinese: his own misreadings

Found while checking the map's translations against Li's BDK translation (`review3_li_*.tsv`). Li was used as a
reader, not an authority; these are places where the Chinese (Taishō 51, no. 2087, CBETA) says otherwise, so his
rendering should not be cited for the point. Ids are the study's.

**Directions.** 01.07.05 千泉 南面雪山 → "north"; 01.07.08 西南行 → "south"; 01.31 西北 → "southwest"; 03.01.12 東北 →
"northwest"; 03.01.16 瞢揭釐城東北 → "northwest"; 03.03 the Indus 南流 → "southwest"; 04.02.03 東南 → "northeast"; 05.03 東南
→ "southwest"; 06.02.12 東南流 → "toward the east"; 07.01.03 西南 → "northeast"; 07.03.16 東南 → "south"; 09.02.16a 西行 →
"northwest"; 09.03.11 西峯石室 → "the east peak"; 10.01.04 次北 → "to the south"; 10.13 南行 → "southwest"; 11.20.02a 西北接拂懍
→ "northeast"; 12.07 北至阿利尼 → "to the east".

**Numbers.** 01.32.01 數百餘 → "a hundred"; 04.02.03 周二十里 → "over twenty"; 05.01 百女 → "one thousand daughters";
07.01.11 曠歲 → "for a year"; 07.03.06 三里 → "three or four li"; 09.02.16 九百九十 → "nine hundred and ninety-nine";
10.14 存者二十餘所 → "only about ten"; 12.09 東西十餘里 → "more than one thousand li" (perhaps a variant).

**Names and terms.** 03.06.10 《釋毘婆沙論》 → "Exposition of the Abhidharma-śāstra"; 08.02.01 女聲樹 read as 女婿; 09.03.01 護月
→ "Candragupta"; 09.03.10 三拘胝大阿羅漢 → "the great arhat Trikoṭi"; 10.01, 10.12.05, 10.13.03 奉以周旋 → "circumambulating"
(it means following the teaching in practice).

**Sense.** 01.07.03 林樹稀疎 → "fruit trees are scarce"; 02.04.14b, 02.04.15 男女 → "sons and wife"; 03.01.03 春夏含凍 dropped,
降神 → "send a deity"; 03.06.08 羅漢、沙彌 → "arhats who were śrāmaṇeras"; 03.06.14 宗親擯逐 → the relatives exiled; 04.01
此國已往 → "formerly"; 04.10.x2 必有所下，我曹厚顏 → "we will boldly face him"; 04.10.x4 斯為不朽 → "an unending task"; 05.01
悅以心競 → "displeased"; 05.01.09 the relic "in the monasteries"; 06.01.04 攝衣而往 → "send some clothes"; 06.02.12 風虛 →
"soil and dust"; 06.04.03, 06.04.x1 莫不沈溺 → "a few were not drowned"; 06.04.14 竹林精舍 → "Jetavana … Rājagṛha"; 07.03.07
鼓濯清流 → "rowing a boat"; 08.02.03 功績咸畢 → "commenced"; 08.06.x2 婆羅門兄弟 → "the younger brother of a brahman"; 09.01.03
崖徑槃薄 → "overgrown with weeds"; 10.12 (Kosala) 門者敬懼，故得入焉 → "the guard had fled", 身命若遺 → "as excrement", the
king's death (果亦命終) dropped; 11.05.03 為座四足 → "a seat with four legs" (the four images are the legs); 11.15 可略而言 →
"of which I had scant information"; 12.22.03 自迦濕彌羅國 dropped.

**Readings of other editions, not errors.** 01.04b "Yanqi" for 烏耆; 01.38 "Suli" for 剎利; 02.04.06 億 as lakh;
09.02.03a, 09.02.16 雨三月 for CBETA's 兩三月 (adopted here).
