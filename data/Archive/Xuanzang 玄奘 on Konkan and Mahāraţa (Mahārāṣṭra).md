Travelogues of contemporary foreign travellers have provided useful information about the Chalukyan empire. The Chinese traveller [Xuanzang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xuanzang) had visited the court of [Pulakeshin II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulakeshin_II). At the time of this visit, as mentioned in the [Aihole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aihole) record, Pulakeshin II had divided his empire into three *Maharashtrakas* or great provinces comprising 99,000 villages each. This empire possibly covered present day [Karnataka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnataka), [Maharashtra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharashtra) and [coastal Konkan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konkan).[40][41] Xuanzang, impressed with the governance of the empire observed that the benefits of the king's efficient administration was felt far and wide. Later, Persian emperor [Khosrau II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khosrau_II) exchanged ambassadors with Pulakeshin II.[42][43][44]
